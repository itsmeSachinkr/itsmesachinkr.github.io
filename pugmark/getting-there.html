Getting There

How to Reach India's National Parks

Pick where you're travelling from and which park you want to visit — we'll suggest a route. For the 12 Maharashtra & Madhya Pradesh reserves we cover in full detail, that includes flight and train options; for every other park, you'll still get the nearest airport and railway station to start planning from.

Flight, train and road guidance here is general knowledge to help you start planning — schedules and connections change. Always confirm current routes and timings with the airline, railway, or a travel operator before booking.

About Pugmark

Pugmark is an independent trip-planning reference for wildlife tourism across India. Always confirm seats, closures and travel schedules with the relevant official source before you travel.
