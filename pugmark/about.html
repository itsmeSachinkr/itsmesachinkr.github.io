About Pugmark

What this site is, what it isn't, and where to actually book a safari.

Pugmark is an independent trip-planning reference for wildlife tourism across India. It exists to answer the three questions every first-time visitor has before booking a safari: what is this park actually famous for, how are its safari zones organised, and roughly how busy will it be on the date I'm planning to go.

Season and demand information is general guidance based on known seasonal patterns — monsoon closures, festival-period demand spikes, and typical weekday/weekend variation. It is not live seat data. Always confirm actual availability, permits, and closures on the relevant state forest department's official portal before you travel.

Full core/buffer zone detail and date-based availability estimates are currently available for parks in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh. Every other state and union territory shows a lighter overview — enough to plan around, but you'll need the official portal or booking counter for zone-level detail and confirmed seats.

Data & Corrections

Park boundaries, zone names and safari lore change over time. If something here looks out of date, cross-check it with the park's official forest department page before you plan around it.
