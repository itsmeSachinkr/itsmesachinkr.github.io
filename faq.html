Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order books through EduKart? ▼
It's simple — your school will share a QR code for your child's class. Just scan it with your phone camera, and your cart automatically fills with the exact books required for that class — right edition, right publisher. Review the list, make payment via UPI, card, or net banking, and your complete book set will be delivered to your home within 48 hours.
How can I be sure I'm getting the correct edition? ▼
That's the core promise of EduKart. Every QR code is configured directly with your school's official book list for the academic year. When you scan, the books that appear are exactly what the school has specified — correct title, correct publisher, correct edition. There is no manual searching involved, which completely eliminates the risk of buying the wrong edition.
What if my school is not yet on EduKart? ▼
You can still reach out to us! Fill out the contact form or call +91 98765 43210 and let us know your school's name and city. We will proactively contact your school's administration to onboard them. The service is completely free for schools — so adoption is quick and easy. You can also ask your school's principal or admin to contact us directly.
Do you deliver JEE and NEET books too? ▼
Yes! JEE and NEET aspirants typically need 25–40 books spread across multiple publishers — which is an exhausting and expensive process to manage alone. EduKart offers curated exam book bundles covering all the required titles in a single order. If your coaching centre is partnered with EduKart, you'll receive a QR on enrolment day. You can also order directly through our platform.
What is included with every order? ▼
Every EduKart order includes the complete physical book set for your class or exam — all subjects, all books, packed together in one box. In addition, every order includes digital eBook access to your full book set, so your child can study from their phone or tablet even before the physical books arrive or in case a book is misplaced. It's the most complete book delivery experience available in India.
Which cities does EduKart currently deliver to? ▼
EduKart is currently launching in Mumbai and will be expanding to more cities very soon. If you're outside Mumbai, fill in the contact form with your city — we're prioritising expansion based on demand. The more people who register interest from a city, the faster we arrive there. We plan to cover 10+ cities across India within the next 2 years.
