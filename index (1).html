FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does EduKart know which books my child needs?
+

When your school partners with EduKart, they share the annual booklist with us and we build a curated bundle for each class. If your school isn't a partner yet, you can manually select books by class, board and subject when registering.

How long does delivery take?
+

Standard delivery takes 2–4 working days across major cities in India. Express delivery (same day or next day) is available in select cities. All orders come with real-time tracking.

Is EduKart available across all of India?
+

We currently serve Mumbai and are rapidly expanding to other major cities across India. Register your interest and we'll notify you as soon as EduKart launches in your city.

Which boards and classes are supported?
+

We support CBSE, ICSE and all major State Boards for Class 1 through Class 12, plus select college programmes. Regional language content is available for 15+ Indian languages.

How does my school partner with EduKart?
+

Fill in the partnership form on this page and our team will contact you within 24 hours. The onboarding process is very simple — just share your booklist and we handle everything else for your students.

Can I get only eBooks without physical delivery?
+

Yes! You can register for eBook-only access and get the full digital textbook library for your class and board. Books can be downloaded for offline reading on any device.
